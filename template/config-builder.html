Build your front desk

Answer a few questions about your shop. No code, no account. You get a config file, and we turn it into your front desk app — booking, reminders, and a dashboard.

Please fix these:

    1. Your business

    The name callers hear, and the hours your front desk books.

    2. Your services

    What customers can book. Prices are the range you quote — leave both blank if you quote after inspection.

    3. How customers reach you

    At least one channel. SMS is first by default — missed calls are reported by text.

    Price estimates need a pricing dataset — available for auto repair.

    Your config file

    This is the whole front desk, in one file. Send it to us and we generate your app — or keep it and run it yourself.
