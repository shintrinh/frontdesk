FrontDesk

This is an online booking page for a dental clinic in the US. New patients can complete the intake — insurance and health history — and book an exam online. No phone call, no account, no payment.

Patients due for recall

Recalls are computed from real past visits — a patient is due when it has been 180 days since their last completed visit and they have no future booking.

Book an appointment

New patients: insurance defaults to Delta Dental and health history to None — change them if needed. Date of birth is optional and can be added later.
